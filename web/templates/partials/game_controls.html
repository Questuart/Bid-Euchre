Help: Bid Euchre Rules

Bid Euchre is a 2 v 2 team game with a 4-player hand of 20 cards: one point-high card (A/K/Q/J/10/9/8/7) per suit plus bowers.

  • Auction: Seat left of dealer starts bidding. Bids begin at 6, then rise.
  • Pass: Enter 0 to pass. All four passes trigger a redeal.
  • Moon bid: A Moon bid is always 10 and can score 20 for success or -20 on fail.
  • Loner: A Loner bid is exactly 10 and removes your partner from tricks.
  • Tricks: First team to 52 points wins the match.
  • High/Low: No-trump bids rank by either high card or low card.
