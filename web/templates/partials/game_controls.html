Help: Bid Euchre Rules

Bid Euchre here is a 4-player, 2 v 2 game using a 40-card double deck: 10, J, Q, K, and A in all four suits, with two copies of each card. Every hand deals all 40 cards, so each player gets 10 cards and the hand lasts 10 tricks.

  • Auction: Bidding starts left of the dealer and goes once around the table. Pass is always legal. If all four players pass, the hand is redealt and the dealer rotates.
  • Contracts: Regular bids name a trick target and either a trump suit or no-trump High/Low. Moon and loner are special level-10 bids.
  • Trick play: Everyone must follow suit if possible. In suit contracts, trump beats every non-trump card. In High/Low, there is no trump.
  • Bowers: In suit contracts, the jack of trump is the right bower and the same-color jack is the left bower. Both count as trump.
  • Duplicates: Because it is a double deck, identical cards can appear in the same trick. If identical winning cards tie, the first one played wins the trick.
  • Moon and loner: Moon triggers a partner exchange before play. Loner makes the declarer's partner sit out the tricks.
  • Scoring: Teams play a running match to +52 or -52. Bid makers score their bid result, defenders score the tricks they take.
